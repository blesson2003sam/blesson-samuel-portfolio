About me

A teacher by profession, a researcher by pursuit, and an engineer who believes antennas belong in cloth as much as in metal.

I serve as an Assistant Professor while pursuing my Ph.D. part-time at the Karunya Institute of Technology and Science, Coimbatore. My doctoral research lies in the design and fabrication of antennas built on e-textiles — antenna structures made from conductive threads and fabrics that can be worn, bent, washed, and lived in.

Alongside my core research, I work with machine learning and deep learning applied to antenna and RF problems, and I build for the web using modern AI-assisted ("vibe coding") development practices — the same way this site itself was made.

What anchors all of it is a conviction: research is only complete when it reaches people. I deliberately steer my work toward societal development — healthcare monitoring, disaster response, and technology access for communities that conventional engineering often overlooks. If an antenna I weave ends up helping one genuinely helpless person, the Ph.D. has already justified itself.

Research

Textile-based antenna engineering, with an explicit bias toward work that helps people.

E-textile antenna fabrication

Designing and fabricating antennas from conductive fabrics and threads — studying how embroidery geometry, substrate textiles, and bending affect radiation performance, bandwidth, and efficiency in real wearable conditions.

Wearable & body-centric communication

Antenna systems integrated into clothing for health monitoring, patient tracking, and continuous on-body communication — where comfort and signal integrity must coexist.

Machine learning for antenna design

Applying deep learning techniques to antenna modelling, optimisation, and parameter prediction — shortening the design loop from simulation to fabricated prototype.

Technology for the underserved

Directing the research toward low-cost, deployable solutions for rural healthcare, disaster relief, and assistive technology — engineering measured by the help it delivers, not only the papers it produces.
